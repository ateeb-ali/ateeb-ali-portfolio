SEC.01

About

I'm a fourth year Cybersecurity student at Dawood University of Engineering & Technology in Karachi. My work sits across four areas: web, mobile, network and infrastructure penetration testing, and digital forensics, mostly built through labs, CTFs, and a cyber risk internship at the State Bank of Pakistan.

I compete in CTFs with a focus on cryptography and OSINT, and I've designed original challenges for Teknofest and a few student-led events rather than just solving them. Alongside the technical side, I've started building a public speaking track record, presenting security topics to students who aren't in cybersecurity themselves, which forces me to explain things clearly instead of relying on jargon.

Outside of cybersecurity, you'll usually find me following football, getting lost in a good film, or exploring something completely unrelated to either.

Currently

Finishing a Cyber Risk Management internship at the State Bank of Pakistan and heading into my final year at DUET, with digital forensics as the area I'm going deepest on.
